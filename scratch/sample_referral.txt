Acme Corp Employee Referral Bonus Program
Effective Date: February 1, 2026

1. Program Overview:
We believe our best talent comes from referrals. Acme Corp offers cash bonuses to employees who refer qualified candidates who are subsequently hired.

2. Bonus Structure:
- Standard Positions (Associate, Analyst): $1,000 bonus
- Specialized Technical Positions (Senior Engineers, Leads): $2,500 bonus
- Executive Positions (Director, VP): $5,000 bonus

3. Payout Schedule:
Referral bonuses are paid in two equal installments:
- 50% paid on the hire's first regular monthly payroll cycle.
- 50% paid after the hire successfully completes their 6-month probationary period.
Both the referring employee and the referred hire must be actively employed by Acme Corp at the time of payout.

4. Eligibility:
All active full-time employees are eligible, except for:
- Members of the HR and Recruiting departments
- Hiring managers for the specific role being filled
- Executive staff (VPs, C-level officers)

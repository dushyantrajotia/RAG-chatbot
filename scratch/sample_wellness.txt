Acme Corp Health and Wellness Benefit Offerings
Effective Date: March 15, 2026

1. Wellness Allowance:
Acme Corp provides a monthly health and wellness allowance of up to $75. This taxable benefit supports physical and mental health activities outside of work.

2. Covered Expenses:
Approved wellness expenses include:
- Gym memberships and fitness class fees
- Personal training sessions
- Registration fees for athletic events (marathons, cycles)
- Mental health app subscriptions (e.g., Headspace, Calm)
- Yoga and meditation class passes

3. Excluded Expenses:
The allowance cannot be used for:
- Medical copays, prescription drugs, or treatment bills
- Dietary supplements, protein powders, or weight loss foods
- Sports clothing, shoes, or recreational equipment
- Spa treatments, massages, or beauty salon services

4. Submissions:
Receipts must be submitted monthly through the corporate benefits portal before the 25th day of the current month. Late submissions will not be paid.

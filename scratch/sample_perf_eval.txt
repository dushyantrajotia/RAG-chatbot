Acme Corp Performance Evaluation and Career Development
Effective Date: June 1, 2026

1. Annual Cycle:
Performance evaluations occur annually between November 1 and December 15. The review process contains three phases: self-evaluation, manager evaluation, and a 1-on-1 review meeting.

2. Rating Scale:
Evaluations are based on a 5-point scale:
- 5: Outstanding (Exceeds all performance targets)
- 4: Commendable (Exceeds most performance targets)
- 3: Effective (Meets core targets consistently)
- 2: Needs Improvement (Fails to meet some targets; requires developmental plan)
- 1: Unsatisfactory (Consistent performance issues; PIP mandatory)

3. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP):
Employees receiving an overall rating of 1 or 2 will be placed on a structured 60-day Performance Improvement Plan. Failure to meet PIP targets may result in termination of employment.

4. Goal Setting:
By January 31, employees and managers must co-author 3 to 5 OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) for the upcoming calendar year, aligned with team and corporate strategies.

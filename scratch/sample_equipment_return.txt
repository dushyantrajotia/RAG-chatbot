Acme Corp Offboarding and IT Asset Return Policy
Effective Date: January 15, 2026

1. Asset Ownership:
All computers, monitors, mobile devices, and office equipment provided to employees remain the exclusive property of Acme Corp.

2. Return Deadlines:
Upon resignation, termination, or contract end, all issued company assets must be returned to the IT department within 5 business days of the employee's final working day.

3. Shipping & Packaging:
- In-Office Employees: Must return items in-person to the IT desk on their final day.
- Remote Employees: Acme Corp will mail pre-paid shipping labels and padded boxes. Remote employees must package and drop off devices at a FedEx location.

4. Non-Compliance:
Failure to return company equipment within the designated 5-day window may result in:
- Retention of final payroll payouts (where legally permitted)
- Charging the employee's account for the fair replacement value of the assets
- Referral to collections agencies or legal counsel

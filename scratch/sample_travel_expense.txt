Acme Corp Corporate Travel and Expense Policy
Effective Date: April 15, 2026

1. General Principles:
Employees traveling on behalf of Acme Corp must exercise prudence and good judgment. Travel must be pre-authorized by the departmental vice president if estimated costs exceed $1,000.

2. Air Travel:
All flights must be booked in Economy class for domestic flights. Business class bookings are permitted only for continuous international flights exceeding 8 hours, subject to prior CFO approval.

3. Lodging & Meals:
- Lodging: Standard hotel rooms up to $200 per night (or $300 in tier-1 high-cost cities like New York, San Francisco, and London).
- Per Diem Meals: Acme Corp offers a daily meal allowance of $75 per day. Receipt submission is not required for per diem claims, but the duration of the trip must be verified.

4. Mileage Reimbursement:
Employees using personal vehicles for business travel are reimbursed at the standard rate of $0.67 per mile. Commutes between home and the primary office location are not eligible for reimbursement.

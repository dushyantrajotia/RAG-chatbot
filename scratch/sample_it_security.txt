Acme Corp IT Security and Data Protection Standards
Effective Date: February 10, 2026

1. Password Complexity Requirements:
All corporate account passwords must be updated every 90 days. Passwords must be at least 14 characters in length and contain:
- At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
- At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
- At least one numerical digit (0-9)
- At least one special symbol (e.g., !, @, #, $, %, ^, &)

2. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):
MFA is mandatory for all access paths to corporate networks, email accounts, and database services. The Okta Verify app is the standard authentication method. SMS-based MFA is restricted.

3. Device Lockout & Inactivity:
Laptops must be configured to automatically lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Entering an incorrect password 5 consecutive times will lock the account for 15 minutes.

4. Data Classification:
Information must be labeled as public, internal-only, or strictly confidential. Confidential customer records must never be stored on local laptop drives; they must remain in protected cloud vaults.

Acme Corp Home Office Stipend Policy
Effective Date: March 1, 2026

1. Benefit Amount:
Acme Corp provides a one-time home office setup stipend of up to $500 (USD) for eligible remote or hybrid employees. This stipend is designed to support ergonomic and productive home working environments.

2. Eligible Items:
The stipend can be used to purchase the following approved items:
- Ergonomic office chairs
- Standing desks or desk converters
- External monitors (up to two)
- Computer keyboards and mice
- Ergonomic footrests or wrist supports

3. Ineligible Items:
Items that cannot be reimbursed under this policy include:
- Home internet bills or installation fees
- Mobile phone handsets or billing plans
- Office decorative items (plants, art, lighting)
- Coffee makers, snacks, or beverages
- Standard laptops and tablets (which are issued directly by IT)

4. Reimbursement Process:
Employees must purchase items first, then submit a claim via the Expensify portal. Submissions must include itemized store receipts. Claims must be submitted within 45 days of the purchase date.

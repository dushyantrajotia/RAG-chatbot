Acme Corp Code of Conduct and Professional Ethics
Effective Date: May 1, 2026

1. Core Values:
Acme Corp is committed to honesty, integrity, diversity, and mutual respect. We expect all team members to act professionally, ethically, and responsibly in all workplace interactions.

2. Harassment and Discrimination:
Any form of harassment, bullying, or discrimination based on race, color, gender, age, religion, national origin, sexual orientation, or disability is strictly prohibited. Acme Corp operates a zero-tolerance policy.

3. Conflicts of Interest:
Employees must avoid situations where their personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of Acme Corp. Engaging in side employment with competitors or vendors is forbidden.

4. Reporting Violations:
Employees who witness ethical breaches or policy violations are encouraged to report them to HR or use the anonymous Ethics Hotline (1-800-555-0199). Retaliation against whistleblowers is not tolerated.
